# rat1 provenance

**Source.** Generated in this repository by `generator/scripts/search_rational.py --seed 4 --samples 15000`, from sample 13077 (the best-ranked candidate with 11 pieces). There is no source archive. The exact function is stored in `recipe.json` under `source.rational`.

**Mathematics.** F = P ⊘ Q, which is P − Q classically, where P and Q are max-convention tropical polynomials of four terms each, over x, y ∈ [−4, 4]. Each piece is a two-dimensional cell of the common refinement: the cell of a term p of P intersected with the cell of a term q of Q. Its roof is the plane p − q, and its id is `p.id + q.id`. P is convex, so its tropical curve becomes valleys; −Q is concave, so Q's curve becomes ridges. The roof is neither a bowl (rev2) nor a set of ridges (new1–new4) but a mixed landscape.

**Search criteria.** The candidate needed 9–16 pieces, none smaller than 3% of the tray, interior angles of at least 20° and edges of at least 8 mm. Its roof tilt had to be at most 30°, so the pieces print roof-down without supports (it is 20.4°). Valley and ridge lengths had to be within a factor of about 3 of each other (here they are almost equal), and no two pieces could be congruent.

**Physical scale.** X = 18·x and Y = 18·y mm, giving a 144 mm square tray. Roof heights, tray rim, flat thickness and tolerances are copied from new2.
