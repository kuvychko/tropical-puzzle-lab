# new1 provenance

**Source.** A min-convention tropical polynomial supplied directly, with no source archive. The exact expression is stored in `recipe.json` under `source.polynomial`. The recipe terms reproduce it exactly, with coefficients kept as fractions.

**Mathematics.** F(x, y) = min of sixteen terms c + a·x + b·y, with a, b ∈ {1, …, 4}, over x, y ∈ [−9, −1] (`domain` 4 around `center` (−5, −5)). Each piece is the region where one term attains the minimum. The relief roof is the graph of F itself. Because F is a minimum of planes it is concave, so the roof rises to ridges rather than falling into a bowl as in rev2.

**Physical scale.** X = 18·(x + 5) and Y = 18·(y + 5) mm, giving a 144 mm square tray, the same size as rev2. Roof heights, tray rim, flat thickness and tolerances are copied from rev2.

**Status.** This is an exploratory puzzle ("16 easy"), still under evaluation.
